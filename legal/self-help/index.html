Where to start if you are handling a legal matter without a lawyer.

Handling a legal matter on your own

You do not need a lawyer to look up a case, read the law, or file with the court. This page points you to the right place to start.

The Department of Attorney General represents the State of Michigan. It generally cannot act as your personal lawyer or give you private legal advice.
