Request public records

You can ask the Department of Attorney General for copies of its public records under the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA).

Making a request

  1. Open the request form.
  2. Describe the records you want. The more specific you are, the faster they can be found.
  3. Give the dates the records cover and the office you think holds them, if you know.
  4. Submit the form and keep your reference number.

Start a FOIA request

Tips for a good request

  • Ask for records, not answers. A FOIA request gets you documents that already exist.
  • Name a time period, a subject, or a case where you can.
  • If you are not sure which office holds the records, choose "Not sure" on the form.
