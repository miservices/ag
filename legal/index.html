Legal services for the State

The Office of the Solicitor General handles all civil matters for the State of Michigan, under the direction of the Attorney General.

Formal opinions are published by the Office of the Attorney General and the Office of the Solicitor General, and from time to time by the Criminal Trials Office as well.

This section also covers public records requests, legal resources, and guidance for people without a lawyer.
