Opinions

Formal opinions on questions of law, published by the Department.

About opinions

A formal opinion answers a question of law. Opinions are generally published by the Office of the Attorney General and the Office of the Solicitor General, and every now and then by the Criminal Trials Office alongside them.

Requesting an opinion

Public officials can ask the Office of the Attorney General for a formal opinion. Send your question to the Department and say which official or agency is asking.
