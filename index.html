Original jurisdiction over criminal prosecutions

The Department of Attorney General retains original jurisdiction for all criminal prosecutions unless a county prosecutor's office has been established. As of now, no county prosecutor's office has been established, so criminal cases are prosecuted by the Criminal Trials Office.
